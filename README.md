# Bits & Tickers: the daily tech, AI and markets podcast

What got built, and what it's worth. Two hosts, Alex and Maya, on tech, AI and markets. First Tick is the day's news in about ten minutes, every morning: the biggest story first, each one told once, named and sourced in its first breath, and checked against its sources before it is said. {dayone}No ads, chapters you can skip through, and a full transcript with every episode. Every story is linked in the show notes. Nothing in this show is financial advice.
