What got built, and what it's worth.

What got built, and what it's worth. Two hosts, Alex and Maya, on tech, AI and markets. First Tick is the day's news in about half an hour, every morning: each story named and sourced in its first breath, and none left without a reason it matters. Day One, on Sundays, tells how one well-known company got from nothing to where it is, then turns that into moves you can make yourself. No ads, chapters you can skip through, and a full transcript with every episode. Every story is linked in the show notes. Nothing in this show is financial advice.
