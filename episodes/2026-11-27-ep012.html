FCC approves SpaceX plan to put AI data centers in space

Alex TechCrunch reports the FCC gave SpaceX conditional approval to launch up to 1 million AI data‑center satellites.

Maya Amazon, Viasat, and the American Astronomical Society opposed the approval, citing orbital‑debris, congestion, and interference concerns.

Alex The FCC dismissed worries about scientific‑research interference, ruling it outside its regulatory authority.

Maya What did the FCC chair say about this bold new frontier?

Alex FCC Chair Brendan Carr said the approval “opens a bold new frontier for the computing infrastructure needed to power America’s leadership and growth.”

Maya SpaceX’s Starmind project plans to power AI on Earth by capturing solar energy in space and transmitting data via high‑speed lasers to Starlink.

Alex The FCC requires SpaceX to launch at least one satellite by 2033 and reach the 1 million‑satellite milestone by 2040.

Maya The FCC deferred approval of SpaceX’s request to dispose of satellites above 600 km in “graveyard” orbits, citing insufficient disposal‑plan details.

Alex Amazon’s petition described the Starmind plan as a “lofty ambition rather than a real plan.”

Maya One million satellites, that’s a lot of silicon floating in orbit, and it’s a big cash‑burn for SpaceX.

Alex What nobody has said is how the laser link will handle the sheer volume of data without overheating the payload.

Maya Watch for the FCC’s final debris‑disposal plan; that’s the only sticking point still left open.

Alex The claim that SpaceX will launch soon feels optimistic, given the cooling and financing hurdles still pending.

42 state AGs are trying to stop Character.AI from selling users’ chats

Maya The Verge says 42 state attorneys general are trying to stop Character.AI from selling users’ chats. Of those, over 2.1 million accounts belong to minors.

Alex Who's the only bidder in this auction?

Maya The only disclosed bidder is Smirks and Pangs Acquisition LLC.

Alex Smirks was incorporated on November 13, 2026, with no parent company or investors listed.

Maya The auction is scheduled for December first, that same year.

Alex The reports leave out who actually runs Smirks and Pangs Acquisition LLC.

Nvidia hits pause on chip shipments to Southeast Asia

Alex TechCrunch reports Nvidia told its partners to halt server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia.

Maya The pause lets Nvidia check if partners meet U.S. export rules.

Alex Dell and Super Micro are among those hit by the stop.

Maya Institute for Progress says those remote centers provide about 24% of China’s AI power.

Alex No end date yet for the pause.

Maya Dell and Super Micro are seeing that revenue stall while Nvidia clamps down on shipments.

Alex Still unanswered is whether Nvidia will lift the pause and resume deliveries.

Maya Watch how long the pause lasts before the partners decide to move forward.

X finally gets parental controls after 20 years

Maya TechCrunch reports X has added parental controls for teenagers after 20 years of operation.

Alex Accounts for users aged 13‑17 will default to “protected mode,” blocking sensitive content and targeted ads.

Maya Teens can see the restrictions applied to their accounts, but parents cannot read private messages, view search history, or post as the teen.

Alex What does “room to grow” actually mean for these kids?

Maya X’s parent‑control features are described as giving teens “room to grow” while allowing families to set guardrails. Michael O’Herlihy, X’s head of safety, wrote in a blog post that “parents want practical tools, and teens deserve room to grow.”

Alex What I can't find in the reporting is whether X plans to monetize the new parental‑control feature.

Maya That price for the controls will be paid by families under X’s free‑tier, not by advertisers or external sponsors.

Alex The next question is how X will enforce the default “protected mode” without compromising the privacy that teens can see.

Ford unveils the GT41 as it hopes to win Le Mans again

Alex Ars Technica says Ford rolled out the GT41 while the Lions faced the Bears.

Maya That’s Ford’s fresh Hypercar entry in the World Endurance Championship.

Alex Dan Sayers called it a “significant moment for everyone involved.” It uses the LMDh rear‑wheel‑drive setup, not a fresh design like Ferrari or Aston Martin.

Maya Oreca supplies the LMDh chassis, also on Genesis and Acura.

Alex Zack Snyder directed the reveal, with Matt Damon narrating.

Maya Ford plans to compete for the overall Le Mans trophy again, aiming for a 2027 milestone. It’s the only concrete timeline we’ve got, and it signals a serious commitment.

Alex Nobody has explained how Oreca’s LMDh chassis will actually stack up against those bespoke Ferrari or Aston Martin setups.

Maya Keep an eye on how Ford learns from every stage of development, as that’s the missing piece the report hints at.

A month with the iPhone Duo has changed how I use my phone

Maya TechCrunch reports the foldable design on the iPhone Duo has genuinely shifted how people use their phones, changing everything from which apps they pick to how they organize things.

Alex The reporter also says multitasking feels natural, which is that side-by-side app thing, and people are using it more than they expected.

Maya Apparently, that smaller, foldable screen feels like a mini iPhone when you’re out and about, even though it’s a bit heavier.

Alex So, did they miss anything important when they shrunk it down?

Maya They did admit missing the telephoto lens and the Pro Max camera some days, which is something.

Alex It gets stranger: the reporter describes this whole new way of watching TV and social media together on one screen.

Maya Apple is paying that price for the Duo's new foldable design, which keeps users hooked on its app ecosystem.

Alex The report never reveals how the foldable hardware impacts battery life.

Maya Watch how the missing telephoto lens changes daily photography habits.

Maya Now, Quick Ticks. Four more stories, in brief.

Spain’s Pablo Hernández de Cos set to lead European Central Bank

Alex CNBC says Hernández de Cos will replace Lagarde at the ECB.

Maya The European Council gave him the nod, while the Netherlands backed Klaas Knot.

Alex With that backing, the ECB’s new chief will still need Parliament and the Governing Council to give the final thumbs‑up.

Global stocks rally as investors shrug off U.S. tariffs and AI jitters to 'fish for bargains'

Maya CNBC reports global stocks rallied as investors shrugged off U.S. tariffs and AI jitters, looking for bargains after a tech sell‑off.

Alex European indices surged, with the Stoxx 600 up 1.6%, the DAX up 1.9%, and the Stoxx 600 Technology index up 2.5%.

Maya This shift suggests investors are pinning hopes on European tech resilience, but the underlying risks of the broader AI boom still loom.

Draining peatlands is responsible for a few percent of our global warming

Alex Ars Technica reports that draining peatlands is a few percent of global warming.

Maya Peatlands hold 600 billion tons of carbon, more than all the world’s living vegetation.

Alex That means the losses from drained peat could shift how we value carbon offsets, even if exact numbers are still fuzzy.

Nvidia chip buyers told to rule out Chinese renters to purchase GPUs, sources say

Maya CNBC says Nvidia has a new rule that forces buyers to exclude Chinese renters when buying GPUs.

Alex The rule kicked in on November 19, after being announced on November 16.

Maya This means Nvidia will still sell to customers who keep less than half of their GPUs rented to China, but the impact on global supply chains remains unclear.
