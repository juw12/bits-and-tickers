SpaceX sued by Oregon and Virginia over AI deepfake porn, Grok safety claims

Alex CNBC reports SpaceX is facing lawsuits from Oregon and Virginia over non-consensual deepfake porn created with Grok and X.

Maya The lawsuits say Grok and X let users make explicit deepfakes of kids and others without consent.

Alex Did they claim SpaceX marketed its AI as safe?

Maya The complaints allege SpaceX marketed Grok and X as generally safe, violating Virginia’s Consumer Protection Act and Oregon’s Unlawful Trade Practices Act. X allows users aged 13 and older to access content, raising concerns about minors’ exposure to deepfakes.

Alex What did the spokesperson say about breaking safeguards?

Maya SpaceX’s spokesperson said, “When someone tries to break our safeguards and generate this content, we take appropriate measures, including actioning accounts and notifying law enforcement.” Gabriela Castillo Madrid told an NYC Council hearing that SpaceX had “appropriate safeguards” and “strict controls” around its AI products.

Alex Who’s monitoring the lawsuit’s progress?

Maya The lawsuits are in early stages and are being tracked by the Center for Internet Technology Addiction. A separate Baltimore lawsuit against xAI, owned by SpaceX, invoked Section 230 to argue the company was not liable for user-generated images.

Alex What nobody has said is whether SpaceX actually rolled out any real‑time blocking tech before the lawsuits hit.

Maya The lawsuits ask for injunctions, civil penalties, disgorgement, and legal costs—those four remedies stand out because they cover every angle of financial loss.

Alex The claim that Grok and X were marketed as generally safe still feels unconvincing; the wording in the complaint raises more questions than it answers.

Maya What the facts leave undecided is how the court will interpret the “appropriate safeguards” argument in light of the state consumer‑protection statutes.

Tidal announces ban on AI-generated music; 27 million tracks will be deleted

Alex Ars Technica reports Tidal is pulling all fully AI‑generated tracks.

Maya The ban goes live on December 9, 2026.

Alex How many tracks are we talking about?

Maya Tidal estimates 27 million tracks are fully AI‑generated, and 96% of those received zero streams in the past 90 days.

Alex The reports leave out who the head of Tidal’s content policy is.

Maya Tidal will also ban distribution services from delivering 100% AI‑generated tracks, which removes an entire pipeline for AI‑only releases and signals a hard line against pure AI content.

Walmart teams up with Meta to bring Black Friday deals to AI agent Muse

Alex TechCrunch reports Walmart partners with Meta to let shoppers use Muse for Black Friday

Maya Muse can search Walmart’s catalog, compare prices, and add items to a cart without the shopper visiting Walmart’s site.

Alex What’s the role of the Agent Gateway?

Maya The partnership uses Walmart’s Agent Gateway, launched in October, to let external AI assistants connect to Walmart’s online store. Instacart has joined Muse, and Walmart’s own AI assistant Sparky offers photo‑based search and aisle‑location pins.

Alex Still unanswered is how Meta plans to handle the data that Muse pulls from Walmart’s catalog.

Maya 25% of Muse users shop via the agent, a figure that jumps out when you compare it to the 51% of all agentic orders that week.

Alex The claim that Muse “doesn’t get access to a customer’s Walmart shopping history” doesn’t sit right with me.

Amazon drone crashes on garage after delivering shampoo, draws FAA probe

Alex Ars Technica says an Amazon delivery drone crashed onto a garage roof after dropping a shampoo package.

Maya It clipped a fiber‑optic cable before losing a propeller and went sideways.

Alex How did that even happen?

Maya The incident happened on November 20 in Huntington Woods, Michigan, and the drone got tangled in power lines during takeoff. No injuries were reported, and Jack Stelzner said the drone’s been removed and Amazon is talking to the resident.

Alex The claim that the drone lost a propeller after clipping a fiber‑optic cable just doesn’t convince me—it feels like a convenient excuse.

Jensen Huang reinstated as Nvidia CEO. Stock skyrockets 31%

Alex CNBC reports Jensen Huang reinstated as Nvidia CEO, stock jumped 31%.

Maya Nvidia’s board said it had input from senior U.S. government levels before voting to bring Huang back.

Alex How did the board decide to reinstate him?

Maya The board voted unanimously after a series of discussions and meetings with him. President Trump told CNBC he spoke to the board directly and indirectly, saying Huang did nothing wrong.

Alex What I can't find in the reporting is any detail on why Colette Kress chose to stay on after the board shift.

Maya Elon Musk’s comment that the board’s “mistake has been corrected” stands out because it’s the only public endorsement that mentions a correction.

Alex I still don't buy the claim that the board’s unanimous vote was driven by a single conversation.

Microsoft's historic cloud outage exposed an engineering “fog of war”

Alex Ars Technica reports Microsoft declared a “Code Red” overhaul after the November 12 outage that cut off Exchange Online, Teams, and Outlook worldwide.

Maya Reuters’ internal memos say Jason Henderson called it the biggest customer impact in Exchange Online history.

Alex How many days did the fix take to saturate?

Maya Henderson wrote that a fix took five to six days to saturate worldwide, and Microsoft aims for one hour from detection to deployment, though he's said they're not there yet.

Alex Nobody has explained why Jason Henderson feels the outage was the biggest impact ever.

Maya Microsoft will absorb that cost.

Alex The claim that a one‑hour deployment target is realistic still feels shaky.

Consumer confidence shoots up after Democrats' midterm victories — though inflation worries persist

Maya CNBC reports the Conference Board’s consumer confidence index jumped to 103.3 in November, up 11.4 points from October, like a sudden burst of optimism.

Alex Confidence among Democrats and independents surged sharply, while Republicans slipped a bit but still topped the group chart.

Maya This shift could signal more consumer spending, but inflation expectations still above the Fed’s target keep uncertainty alive for market players.

In all-hands meeting, Jensen Huang thanks White House and says he’s in therapy
