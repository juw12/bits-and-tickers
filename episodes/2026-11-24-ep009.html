AI adoption grows in Asia, but measuring the gains remains tricky

Alex CNBC says AI adoption’s on the rise across Asia‑Pacific, and firms are actually seeing returns.

Maya Techcombank in Vietnam used AI to assess loan applications, improving digital banking infrastructure with AWS, which cut new product launch time by 30%. That's a big win for a bank that usually moves slowly, and it shows the point of real deployment.
