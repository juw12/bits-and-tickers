Maya TechCrunch reports 1Password confirmed a hacker accessed its internal systems on November 19, but customer vaults were not compromised.

Maya The attacker also obtained public keys and account/user identifiers.

Alex 1Password says it has no access to customer passwords or Secret Keys that encrypt vaults.

Maya The company disabled the compromised GitHub account, rotated internal credentials, and added a new detection system.

Alex What I can't find in the reporting is whether the employee who logged in from that GitHub account was a contractor or a full‑time developer.

Maya 1Password said it disabled the compromised GitHub account and rotated internal credentials, which shows the company acted quickly, but that speed alone doesn’t explain how the breach happened.

Alex The next thing to watch is whether the new detection system will catch a similar attack before it spreads.
